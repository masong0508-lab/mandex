# Walking parry rewards and direct browser gadget picker

All ports: +150 points per real briefcase deflection and +1 second of walking time up to six times per life. The time cap remains 60 seconds; an expired life cannot revive, and inactive projectiles cannot score twice. A new walking life resets its budget; recovery/tubes do not. The native event carries the actual awarded time to DS, 3DS and Dreamcast. Campaign IDs, option IDs and save serialization are unchanged.

Browser: tap TOOL or press T while aiming to see all eight selections at once. Live stock, descriptions and disabled unavailable tools; no charges spent by selecting. The modal pauses the simulation, owns its touch, and requires a matching down/up within the same card. Cancelled or foreign touches cannot launch or select. Switching away from the air valve safely restores the sling. Charged soda is protected. Native toolbar controls are unchanged.

Validation: production-source browser picker and parry tests, four supported layouts, stock and modal input checks. Native tests and the existing CI packaging gates validate the shared combat changes. Hardware and Chromium full-game verification remain separate.
