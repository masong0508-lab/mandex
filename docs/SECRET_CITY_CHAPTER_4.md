# Secret City: Chapter 4
The 1,110-stage campaign's final 100 numbered levels (1011–1110) form Chapter 4. After completing level 1010 the player can access the otherwise-hidden level 1099 as a standalone qualifying mission. Clearing all six of its sectors earns a completion star, reveals the chapter, and opens its first uncleared level (starting at 1011). Level 1099 counts as already cleared. This avoids an impossible circular unlock: the qualifying mission alone is available early. The standard unlock cheat does not bypass the gate. Chapter visibility is derived from the existing persisted stars, so no save-format change or new flag is required. The 18-sector finale remains level 1110.
 
The combined expansion preserves the geometry of the existing 660-level main branch. It adds 987 entrances to the next 439 layouts and fourteen skyline bridges in the seven metro maps. Geometry validation covers 1176 entrances in total, the new bridges, and 43 finale tube routes. The packed shared data falls from 399355 to 389295 bytes. Cached city/mountain backdrop continuity and bounded life HUD rendering reduce unnecessary work. Browser tests cover secret tab visibility, the qualifier, pagination and earlier camera controls. Native CI and full hardware verification must run before merging.
